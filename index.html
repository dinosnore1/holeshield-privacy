Hole Shield

DNS filtering for Android TV, Google TV and phones.

Hole Shield blocks ads, trackers and TV telemetry at the DNS layer, on the device. It runs a local DNS-only VPN, its own blocklists, query log, statistics and an optional web dashboard. No root required. No accounts, analytics or ads in the app.

Privacy policy Source and APK builds

What it does

Filter every app

A local VpnService routes DNS through the embedded resolver. Video and downloads are not routed through a remote server.

Gravity-style lists

Use the curated maximum preset or your own hosts/adblock URLs, with exact, wildcard and regex allow/deny rules.

Dashboard and stats

Browse the query log and counters at http://DEVICE-IP:8080/admin/. Optional LAN DNS listens on port 5354.

TV and phone

D-pad and touch layouts, dark Material 3 theming, and optional start at boot.
