Hole Shield — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to Hole Shield for Android, Android TV, iPhone and iPad

Short version: Hole Shield filters DNS on your device. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and the developer does not collect or receive your DNS queries, query history, device identifiers or location. Query history stays in the app's private storage on your device.

1. Who we are

Hole Shield is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: gaganpachory@gmail.com.

2. How the app works

On Android, Hole Shield uses the VpnService API to create a local, DNS-only VPN on your device. On iPhone and iPad it uses Apple's Network Extension (NEDNSProxyProvider) as a local DNS proxy. Neither mode connects to a remote VPN server operated by us, and neither carries your browsing, video, downloads or general network traffic. The operating system routes DNS lookups through the app so they can be checked against blocklists before they are resolved.

3. Information the app processes on your device

  • DNS queries and answers. Domain names, query types, response codes and timestamps are processed locally so lists and rules can be applied. A local query log (domain, time and client address or app) and counters are stored in the app's private storage or, on iOS, in its shared App Group container. You can clear them from the app or by clearing app data.
  • Settings and rules. Your preferences, allow/deny rules, blocklist URLs, web username/password and pause state are stored in the app's private storage or, on iOS, in its shared App Group container.
  • Network information. The app reads the device's local IP address to show the dashboard/LAN DNS address. This information is not sent to us.
  • Blocklists. The app downloads lists from the URLs you or the app's preset specify. The list provider receives the request (including your IP address) directly, as with any download.

This information is not uploaded to us. We have no server that receives it and no ability to read it.

4. DNS resolution and third parties

DNS queries that are not blocked are sent to the upstream resolver you choose. By default this is Cloudflare's DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint (https://cloudflare-dns.com/dns-query). If encrypted DNS fails, or if you select plain DNS, queries may be sent unencrypted to a public resolver such as 1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8 or 9.9.9.9.

Those resolver operators process the queries under their own privacy policies. We do not receive a copy. You can change the DoH URL or turn the upstream off in Settings.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social SDKs or crash reporting SDKs.

5. Web dashboard and LAN DNS

The dashboard and LAN DNS listener are off by default. If you enable them, other devices on your local network can reach the app over plain HTTP on the configured port (dashboard default 8080, LAN DNS default 5354). Setting a web username/password restricts dashboard and API access, but traffic is not encrypted. Enable these features only on a network you trust.

On iPhone and iPad, the dashboard and LAN DNS listener run only while the app is in the foreground. They stop when iOS suspends the app. The DNS proxy continues filtering after the app is suspended.

When the dashboard is enabled, the app stores the web login in private preferences. It is not encrypted at rest.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: download blocklists and resolve DNS queries.
  • VpnService (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): run the local DNS-only VPN on Android.
  • Network Extension (DNS proxy): run the local DNS filter on iPhone and iPad.
  • Foreground service: keep filtering active while the Android app is in the background, with an ongoing notification.
  • Notifications: show the required foreground-service notification and show protection status on Android.
  • Receive boot completed: optionally resume protection after a restart on Android, if you enabled "Start at boot".
  • Local Network (iOS): optionally serve the dashboard or LAN DNS listener to devices on your Wi-Fi while the app is open.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We do not collect personal data from the app. The only network requests the app makes are blocklist downloads and DNS queries to the resolver you configure, plus optional dashboard/LAN DNS traffic on your own network.

8. Data retention and deletion

Query history, settings and rules remain on your device until you clear them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because we never receive this data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. To remove all app data, uninstall Hole Shield, use Android's Settings → Apps → Hole Shield → Storage → Clear storage, or use Reset local data in the iOS app's Settings screen.

9. Security

App data is kept in the app's private storage on Android and in its shared App Group container on iOS. Blocklist downloads and DoH queries use HTTPS. The local dashboard uses HTTP and is disabled by default. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and a rooted or jailbroken device or a full backup can expose app data.

10. Children

Hole Shield is a general-purpose network utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
